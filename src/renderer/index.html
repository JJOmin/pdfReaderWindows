    Voices

    Each language has a main voice. Quotes and headings can use their own.

    Fades each sentence in and out over 8 ms and removes sound below 70 Hz. It changes no word timing. Takes effect from the next sentence.

    Phone sync

    Prepares the open document for the phone: every sentence is spoken with the chosen voices and stored on this computer, with the PDF.

    Phones on this network

    ⓘ

    The phone and this computer have to be on the same network, and the network has to let them talk to each other.

    A VPN usually stops it. The phone still finds this computer, but the answers go into the VPN's tunnel and never reach the phone. Switch the VPN off on this computer and on the phone while they sync, or allow access to the local network in the VPN's settings (some call it split tunnelling or "bypass").

    Guest Wi-Fi and networks that keep devices apart block it too. Sync through Nextcloud or Dropbox, below, works on any network.

    Paired phones only, and only after you accept them here. Switch it off on networks you do not trust.

      Sync between devices

      Keeps the prepared documents and your place in your own Nextcloud or Dropbox. Everything is encrypted here first, so the service sees only scrambled files.

      Sync key

      The key that scrambles the files. Enter the same key on every device. Keep a copy: without it the files in the store cannot be read.
